# Gucci Group — Company Overview & Strategy

## About Gucci Group (Kering)

Gucci Group is part of Kering, a global luxury conglomerate headquartered in Paris, France.
Kering owns a portfolio of prestigious luxury brands including Gucci, Saint Laurent, Bottega Veneta,
Balenciaga, Alexander McQueen, and others.

## Mission & Vision

**Mission**: To empower imagination through the most desirable luxury products and experiences.

**Vision**: A Group where each brand maintains its unique creative DNA while benefiting from
shared capabilities, talent pools, and operational excellence.

## Gucci Brand DNA

Gucci is defined by its bold creativity, Italian craftsmanship, and cultural relevance.
The brand sits at the intersection of heritage and modernity.

Key brand pillars:
- Creative audacity and boundary-pushing design
- Italian artisanal excellence
- Cultural dialogue and diversity
- Sustainability commitment (Gucci Equilibrium)

## Group Structure — Brand Autonomy vs. Group Cohesion

One of the central tensions in the Group's operating model is balancing:

**Brand Autonomy**: Each brand has its own Creative Director, CEO, and HR leadership.
They make independent decisions about brand positioning, hiring, and culture.

**Group Cohesion**: Kering provides shared services (finance, legal, sustainability, talent mobility)
and defines Group-level frameworks (e.g., the Competency Framework) that apply across all brands.

The principle: "Supporting, not imposing."
