# APAC Regional Insights — Gucci Group

## Regional Overview

The APAC (Asia Pacific) region is one of Gucci Group's highest-growth markets,
with key hubs in Tokyo, Shanghai, Seoul, Singapore, and Sydney.

## Talent Landscape

### Strengths
- Strong pipeline of luxury retail talent in Japan and South Korea
- High educational attainment and work ethic across the region
- Growing pool of bilingual (English + Asian language) senior leaders

### Challenges
- Cultural tension between "entrepreneurship" (Western individualism) and
  Confucian collectivist workplace norms — particularly in Japan, China, Vietnam
- High attrition among Gen-Z talent in Singapore and Australia (28% annual avg)
- Brand silos: Gucci, Balenciaga, and Saint Laurent APAC teams rarely share talent
- Language barriers complicate Group-wide internal communications

## Competency Framework Rollout Status (APAC)

**Current adoption**: 43% of APAC managers trained (target: 85%)

**Key blockers**:
1. **Module alignment dispute** (HRM-003): APAC HR leads disagree on whether
   "Entrepreneurship" competency should be adapted for collectivist cultures or
   applied uniformly. Blocked since Q4 2024.
2. **Translation quality**: Current Mandarin and Japanese translations of the
   framework documents are flagged as "too literal" — losing cultural nuance
3. **Training delivery**: Live workshops preferred over e-learning in Japan;
   budget not yet approved for in-person delivery

## Inter-Brand Mobility (APAC)

- Only 8 cross-brand transfers occurred in APAC in the past 12 months (target: 25)
- Main barrier: Brand leads reluctant to "lose" high-potential employees
- Pilot program proposed: "APAC Talent Exchange" — 6-month rotations between brands
  Status: Awaiting CEO sign-off

## Recommended Interventions

1. **Cultural adaptation workshop**: Co-design APAC-specific behavioral examples
   for each competency with regional HR leads
2. **Leadership communication**: CEOs of each APAC brand publicly endorse mobility
3. **Incentive realignment**: Include "talent sent to mobility" as a KPI for brand HRDs
