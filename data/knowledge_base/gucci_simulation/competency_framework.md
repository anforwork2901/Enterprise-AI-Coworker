# Gucci Group Competency Framework

## Overview

The Gucci Group Competency Framework defines the core behaviors expected of all leaders
and individual contributors across the Group's brands. It serves as the foundation for:
- Talent assessment and promotion decisions
- Learning & development program design
- Inter-brand mobility evaluation
- Performance calibration

## The Four Core Competencies

### 1. Vision
*Seeing beyond the immediate and thinking long-term.*

Behavioral indicators:
- Anticipates market trends and consumer shifts
- Connects day-to-day decisions to long-term brand and Group strategy
- Inspires others with a compelling picture of the future
- Navigates ambiguity with confidence

### 2. Entrepreneurship
*Acting with the initiative and agility of a founder.*

Behavioral indicators:
- Identifies and seizes opportunities before being directed
- Takes calculated risks and learns from failure rapidly
- Challenges the status quo with constructive alternatives
- Operates effectively with limited resources

### 3. Passion
*Bringing authentic energy and commitment to craft and brand.*

Behavioral indicators:
- Demonstrates deep knowledge and love for the brand's heritage and products
- Goes beyond the expected in quality and attention to detail
- Energizes teams through genuine enthusiasm
- Connects emotionally with clients and collaborators

### 4. Trust
*Building reliable, authentic relationships at every level.*

Behavioral indicators:
- Acts with integrity and consistency between words and actions
- Creates psychological safety for teams to express ideas and concerns
- Follows through on commitments, escalates blockers early
- Respects confidentiality and handles sensitive information with discretion
